# ABC simulations

Curated materials imported from the `vincent_abc_warm_start` branch.

## Contents

- `notebooks/`: current ABC notebooks plus small CSV inputs they read locally.
- `scripts/`: terminal runner for the MU284 ABC experiment.
- `populations/`: real, simulated, and stored population CSVs used by the ABC work.
- `artifacts/`: selected final-looking figures, tables, reports, and paper fragments.

## Notes

Older exploratory notebooks from `simulations_abc/store/` were not copied into
this curated main-branch layout. Generated logs, checkpoint/error CSVs, and
binary kernel/state dumps were also left out.

Absolute local paths that pointed at `simulations_abc` were updated to point at
`simulations/abc`.
