Translation and grid-projected rotation for a fixed 20 × 20 population (N = 400, n = 20). Panels show (a) the original sample, (b) translation by (1/19, −1/19), and targets rotated (c) 90° and (d) 45° counterclockwise about the sample center, projected to distinct population-grid units. Colored points and pale polygons show the saved cluster configurations. Red circles denote sampled units, hollow circles their original positions, black crosses the medoids, teal arrows the movement, and dashed lines the medoid-to-sample matching. Plotted configurations and displayed index values are retained from the original saved figure; this is a layout change only.
