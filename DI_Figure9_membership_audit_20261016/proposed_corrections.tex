The population is
\(U=\{(i/19,j/19):i,j=0,\ldots,19\}\), and the original sample is
\(\{1,5,9,13,17\}\times\{2,7,12,17\}/19\).
The rotation targets in Figure~\ref{fig:rotation_4x5} are generated about
the sample mean \((9/19,1/2)\) and then assigned to distinct grid units.
Thus all displayed configurations are feasible samples, but the
\(45^\circ\) panel represents a grid approximation: its unprojected
targets are all off-grid and four lie outside the population boundary.
The displayed \(90^\circ\) configuration also admits an exact rotation
description, about \((9/19,10/19)\).
Equality of the displayed DI values in panels~(a)--(c) concerns these
particular samples; panel~(d) does not isolate the effect of an exact
\(45^\circ\) rotation.
