Paper-style version of the eight verified saved spatial panels.

colored population points give the actual memberships. Point area is proportional
to the population unit inclusion probability on the same scale in every panel.
Population markers enlarged to make unequal inclusion probabilities more visible;
area = 12*pi_i/(8/144) square points. Eta is omitted from panel titles.

Top row shift: (1,1) raw grid steps; bottom row shift: (9,8).
Values displayed to four decimal places; full precision retained in panels.json
and the original saved results. No fits or scientific calculations rerun.
