Sample translation under equal (EP) and unequal (UP) inclusion probabilities for a fixed 12 × 12 population (N = 144, n = 8). The top and bottom rows show Scenario 1 and Scenario 2, with sample shifts of (1, 1) and (9, 8) grid steps, respectively. Columns show the original and shifted samples under EP, followed by the original and shifted samples under UP. Coordinates are normalized to [0, 1]. Colored points identify population units and their cluster memberships; pale polygons show cluster convex hulls. Population-point areas are proportional to inclusion probabilities, which remain attached to the fixed population units. Red circles mark sampled units, black crosses mark IPnM medoids, and dashed lines show the medoid-to-sample matching. Each panel uses an independently fitted reference. ΔDI = DI_shifted − DI_original denotes the change in DI within each scenario and probability setting. Values are displayed to four decimal places.
